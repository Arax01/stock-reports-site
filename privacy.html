Stock Research

A personal research tool. Not a product, not a service, and not available to anyone else.

Stock Research is a private program that one person runs for their own stock research. It writes research reports and screens from public market data and public SEC filings.

Its only use of Google is to copy those reports into a folder in its owner's own Google Drive, so the owner can read them there. It asks for the narrowest Drive permission, drive.file: it can see and change only the files it created itself, and nothing else in the Drive.

  • No other Google data is read (no email, contacts, calendar or other Drive files).
  • Nothing is shared, sold or sent to anyone else.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
